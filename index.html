For My Favourite Didi ❤️

Tap to open.

20 May 2025.

Jab main first time office aaya tha,
tab mujhe bilkul nahi pata tha ki ye jagah
mujhe sirf ek job nahi,
ek aisa rishta bhi degi jo mere liye itna important ho jayega.

First month mujhe kuch samajh nahi aa raha tha.
Har step par aapne samjhaya, guide kiya—
kabhi senior bankar, kabhi mentor bankar,
aur kabhi bilkul Didi ki tarah, bina irritate hue.

Dheere-dheere pata hi nahi chala
kab sirf work ki baaton se
personal life aur family tak ki baatein hone lagi.
Kab aapse baat karna routine se zyada
apnapan ban gaya.

Aur shayad isi liye May aur June ka woh waqt aaj bhi itna yaad aata hai.

Kabhi-kabhi poora din baatein hoti thi, aur phir bhi lagta tha ki baatein khatam hi nahi hui. Us waqt shayad humne socha bhi nahi hoga ki ye normal se lagne wale moments ek din itni khaas memories ban jayenge.

Phir 11 July 2026 ke baad dheere-dheere baatein kam hone lagi. Aaj bhi nahi pata ki aisa kyun hua. Shayad circumstances badle, shayad routine badal gaya, ya shayad waqt ke saath cheezein pehle jaisi nahi rahin.

Kabhi-kabhi sochta hoon ki jo baatein pehle itni aasani se ho jaati thi, woh ab kyun nahi hoti. Lekin har badlaav ka koi ek jawab ho, zaroori toh nahi.

Mere liye aap hamesha woh senior rahengi, jinke saath office aate-aate ek aisa rishta ban gaya jo sirf office tak limited nahi raha — aap meri Didi ban gayin. ❤️

Pata nahi main aapka best junior tha ya nahi,
aur office boy wala title toh shayad mil hi sakta hai.
But honestly—
You will always be my favourite Senior,
and more importantly, my Didi. ❤️

Aapki life ka ab ek naya chapter shuru ho raha hai.
Meri dil se wish hai ki aage ka har step
aapko khushi, success aur sukoon de.

Thank you for understanding me,
for guiding me,
and for being a part of my life
that I will always remember with a smile.

20 May 2025 se shuru hui ye journey
sirf ek office memory nahi hai—
mere liye ye ek aisa bond hai
jise main hamesha dil se respect karunga. ❤️

Aur haan Didi, agar kabhi meri kisi harkat se aap pareshan hui ho, toh uske liye sorry. Waise junior tha, thodi bahut nautanki aur galtiyan toh banti hi thi! Bas umeed hai meri wajah se aapke face par jitni baar smile aayi ho, woh meri galtiyon se zyada ho. ❤️

Thank you for everything, Didi. ❤️
